Privacy Policy

Auto Daak Collector is a small internal tool used by the school education department, Layyah district, Punjab, Pakistan. It is operated by Muhammad Waqas. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What the app does

The app detects Google Sheets links, reads the structure and existing values of those spreadsheets, prepares proposed updates from school records, and lets an administrator review them. Nothing is written to a spreadsheet unless an administrator explicitly approves the exact changes.

Google user data

The app accesses Google Sheets using the operator's own Google account and read-only permission (spreadsheets.readonly) for reading. A separate write permission is used only to apply changes that an administrator has approved. The app reads only spreadsheets that the connected account can already open.

What is stored

Spreadsheet links, spreadsheet IDs, titles, worksheet names, column headings, proposed and approved changes, and audit records of who approved what and when. Authentication tokens are kept in protected server-side secret storage and are never published.

Sharing and use

Data is used only to operate this tool. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept for operational and audit purposes. You can request deletion of data related to you by emailing the contact below.
